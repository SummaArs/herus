  The tolerance is asymmetric, and that is a property of the radio rather
  than of the schedule: the receiver may open its 20 ms window anywhere
  from 20 ms BEFORE the sender starts to 32.8 ms after it, because a frame
  whose preamble is still running is still detectable. At 40 us/s of
  relative drift that band is worth about 500 s of silence in the worst
  direction — so BEAT_RESYNC_MS = 60 s is not a comfort setting, it is the
  deadline past which a silent group stops being able to hear each other,
  with roughly 8x of headroom.

S4. birthday — what E-P2 cost, in collisions per peer
-------------------------------------------------------
  E-P2 spent two address bits on ttl: 14 bits of address, 32 live per
  session. A frame for peer X can land in the window of peer Y, and the
  cost is one wasted AEAD over 24 bytes plus one rate-limiter token.

   peers   live addrs   P(a random frame hits some window)   measured
       2          64                            0.0039     0.0040
       4         128                            0.0078     0.0075
       8         256                            0.0155     0.0151
      16         512                            0.0308     0.0303
      32        1024                            0.0606     0.0607

  At 32 peers that is one wasted decryption per 17 foreign frames.
  A 24-byte AEAD costs ~16 us, so even at the band's ceiling of 4.1
  frames/s the wasted work is 0.0004 % of one core. E-P2's bill is real
  and it is small — the honest conclusion is that it was worth paying.
  PASS  the measured collision rate matches the birthday model
  PASS  even at 32 peers, fewer than 1 in 10 foreign frames costs a decryption

  symbol; a short press accepts, a long press asks for the next. A
  symbol offered at rank k therefore costs k+1 presses, and the whole
  job of the cognition layer is to put the right one at rank 0.

  4000 messages, trained on the first 2800, measured on the last 1200.

   model     presses/message   worst message   vs no model
   flat                13.89              32        1.0x
   unigram             12.06              41        1.2x
   context              9.44              34        1.5x
   top-2 whole          7.74              22        1.8x
